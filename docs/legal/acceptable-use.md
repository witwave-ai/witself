# Witself Acceptable Use Policy

> **DRAFT — not yet in force.** Skeleton drafted for owner and counsel
> review; not legal advice. Bracketed items are decisions or facts the owner
> must confirm. This page becomes binding only when ratified and published.

Witself stores durable agent state. You may not use it to:

- break the law, or store/distribute content you have no right to hold;
- attack, probe, or overload the Service or anyone else's systems (no
  credential stuffing, malware C2, phishing infrastructure, or spam relays —
  including via agent email);
- send unsolicited bulk email through agent email, forge sender identity, or
  attempt to bypass SPF/DKIM/DMARC enforcement;
- attempt to access other tenants' data or bypass plan limits, sandboxes, or
  rate limits;
- resell the managed Service as your own competing hosted service (the source
  license's competing-use restriction is separate and governs the code);
- store material involving the exploitation of minors, or content whose
  possession is itself unlawful — reported where legally required.

Abuse backstops (rate limits, size bounds, storage ledgers) are part of the
Service. We may throttle, suspend, or close accounts per the Terms; where
practicable we contact you first. Report abuse to [confirm: abuse@witwave.ai].

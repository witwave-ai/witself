# Refunds & Cancellation

> **DRAFT — not yet in force.** Skeleton drafted for owner and counsel
> review; not legal advice. Bracketed items are decisions or facts the owner
> must confirm. This page becomes binding only when ratified and published.

- **14-day refund, first charge.** The charge that starts a paid subscription
  is refundable in full for 14 days from the charge date, on request —
  contact support. A refund ends paid service immediately; the account
  continues on the free plan and data is retained per the
  [data-retention policy](../data-retention-policy.md).
- **Renewals** are not automatically refundable; ask support — goodwill
  refunds of a recent renewal are at our discretion.
- **Cancel anytime** in-product. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the
  paid period; you keep paid features until then, then the account continues
  on the free plan. No partial-period proration.
- **Failed payments**: renewals are retried automatically; if payment
  ultimately fails the subscription is cancelled and the account continues on
  the free plan. Nothing is deleted for non-payment.
- One refund per account per plan. Disputes/chargebacks supersede refunds.

Operator-side procedure: [refund runbook](../refund-runbook.md).
